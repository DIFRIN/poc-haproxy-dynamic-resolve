FINDING — CURRENT ARCHITECTURE, CONFIRMED VULNERABILITY

  Production Squid applies NO destination validation. Any client that can
  reach it may CONNECT to loopback, to RFC1918, and to the cloud
  instance-metadata address 169.254.169.254. 'http_access deny to_localhost'
  is unreachable: both allows that precede it are unrestricted by source.

  This is the production configuration, not a POC artefact. It is why
  TARGET's resolved-address policy is net-new security capability rather
  than a re-implementation of something CURRENT already had.
